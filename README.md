# Customer Churn Report for Telecom Company - Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Customer Churn Report for Telecom Company** is an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze customer churn, revenue, and customer behavior in the telecom industry.

The dashboard helps business management understand **why customers are leaving, which subscription plans and regions have higher churn, and how churn impacts recurring revenue**.

The project uses **Google BigQuery as the data source** and Power BI for data transformation, modeling, analysis, and visualization.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Identify customer churn across different subscription plans
- Analyze customer churn trends over time
- Identify regions with the highest customer churn
- Understand complaint types associated with customer churn
- Analyze the impact of churn on revenue
- Identify revenue lost due to customer churn
- Support customer retention strategies
- Enable data-driven business decision-making

## 🏗️ Data Architecture

A **Star Schema** data model was designed using fact and dimension tables to improve reporting performance and simplify business analysis.

### Data Model

The model consists of:

- Fact tables containing customer and business transaction information
- Dimension tables containing customer, plan, region, date, and complaint-related information

The star schema helps improve query performance and provides a structured approach for analytical reporting.

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard provides interactive analysis across multiple dimensions.

### Customer Churn by Subscription Plan

Analyzes churn across different subscription plans to identify plans with higher customer attrition.

### Churn by Region

Identifies geographical regions with higher customer churn and helps management investigate regional customer behavior.

### Churn Trends Over Time

Analyzes monthly churn trends to identify changes in customer retention over time.

### Churn by Complaint Type

Analyzes customer complaints and their relationship with churn to help identify potential service issues affecting customer retention.

### Revenue Analysis

Analyzes revenue generated by customers and estimates the financial impact of customer churn.

### Revenue Lost to Churn

Measures the revenue associated with customers who have churned, helping management understand the financial impact of customer attrition.

## 🔄 Interactive Features

The dashboard includes several Power BI interactive features:

- **Slicers** for dynamic filtering
- **Drill Down** for hierarchical analysis
- **Drill Through** for detailed customer-level analysis

- Interactive charts and KPI cards

These features allow users to explore the data dynamically and identify important business trends.
